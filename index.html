01 — About

How I work

I turn fragile monoliths into resilient, observable pipelines. Most of my work sits where throughput, correctness and compliance meet: financial data that has to be right, fast, and auditable.

  1. Design for the failure mode

    Traffic spikes, partial outages and bad input will happen. Guardrails and observability ship with the first version, not after the first incident.

  2. Measure, then optimise

    Query plans, latency percentiles and profiler output decide the redesign. The 92.5% and 50% wins came from data, not guesses.

  3. Ship systems people can trust

    From JWT-gated gateways to LLM guardrails in a regulated environment, reliability and safety are requirements, not features.
